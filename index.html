How to play
  1. Start anywhere in the top row.
  2. Your next pick must be in the same column. The one after that, in the same row. Keep alternating.
  3. Each cell works once. Every pick goes into the buffer.
  4. A target counts when its codes sit side by side in the buffer, in order.
  5. Upload all three targets before the buffer fills. Daily puzzle: three tries.
